Contemporary Sesame Broccoli and Bell Pepper Salad

Prep: 12 minutes · Cook: 3 minutes · Total: About 20 minutes · Serves 4 as banchan

Ingredients

The vegetables

  • 400 g broccoli, cut into flat-topped florets (the flat cut sears/blanches evenly)
  • 1 red or yellow bell pepper (about 150 g), cut into batons

The dressing

  • 1.5 tbsp toasted sesame oil (about 22 ml)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce (15 ml)
  • 1 tsp honey (about 5 g)
  • 1 clove garlic (about 4 g), grated
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds (about 8 g)
  • A pinch of gochugaru (about 1 g), optional
  • 1 scallion (about 15 g), sliced; black pepper

Instructions

  1. The blanched, dried broccoli and the raw pepper in the serving bowl, the dressing poured over — sesame oil, soy, honey, garlic — and tossed with spoons for thirty seconds. Checkpoint — every floret glossed, the sesame seeds caught in the florets' crowns (the flat-top cut's doing), the pepper's raw crunch intact beside the broccoli's blanched-firm.
  2. Scallion and gochugaru over, one more turn, and the bowl either serves now or lides and chills. Checkpoint — the eating test: sesame-forward, the broccoli's green sweetness, the pepper's bright crunch — a bowl that tastes like the modern Korean lunch café's cold-case, which is precisely its lineage and its honest seat at this grill table.

Storage, Make-Ahead & Variations

- The make-ahead exception: keeps 2 days refrigerated, the Florets holding snap — refresh with a few drops of sesame oil and a re-toss at serving. - Variations: blanched green beans for broccoli; matchstick carrot for pepper; a spoon of cho-gochujang (#SA03) swirled into the dressing for the sweet-heat fork.
