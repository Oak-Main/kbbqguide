Garlic-Scallion Chicken Skewers

Korean · Easy · Serves 4 (makes 12 skewers)

Jump to Recipe Method Troubleshooting Storage
**Stream: restaurant.** Skewers are the grill's oldest form on the peninsula — the jeok that sat at the center of the ceremonial table — and this record is the tabletop's weeknight version of that idea: chicken thigh cubes and scallion segments on a stick, in a garlic-soy-sesame marinade light enough that the scallion survives the fire. The skewer is not decoration here; alternating meat and scallion means every bite carries the allium's sweetness against the chicken fat.

Garlic-Scallion Chicken Skewers

Prep: 25 minutes, plus 1–3 hours marinating · Cook: 10 minutes · Total: About 40 minutes (marinate ahead) · Serves 4 (makes 12 skewers)

Ingredients

The skewers

  • 800 g boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 3 cm cubes
  • 2 bundles scallions (about 120 g), whites and pale greens cut into 3 cm segments
  • 12 bamboo skewers, soaked 30 minutes (or metal)

The marinade

  • 60 ml soy sauce
  • 1.5 tbsp honey (about 30 g)
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil (15 ml)
  • 1 tbsp mirin (15 ml)
  • 6 cloves garlic (about 24 g), grated — the garlic leads here
  • 1 thumb ginger (about 10 g), grated
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper (about 2 g)
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds (about 4 g)

The brush sauce

  • 2 tbsp of the reserved marinade + 1 tbsp melted butter or extra sesame oil (15 ml)

The table

  • Steamed rice (about 500 g cooked), cho-gochujang (#SA03) on the side as the dip, kimchi (#B01)

Instructions

  1. Medium-hot fire, skewers laid across with a finger's width between them. First side undisturbed 3 minutes — the cubes need to release, and the scallion wants one face charred softly. Checkpoint — turn a test skewer: the chicken's underside should be browned with grill lines and release cleanly; the scallion's face should be charred at the tips and glossy in the middle. If the scallion is incinerated, the fire is a notch hot for its tolerance — slide the scallion ends toward the fire's cooler edge on the next round.
  2. Turn every 2 minutes through three more faces, brushing with the sauce on the second turn — 8–10 minutes total. The corners of the cubes will take little char-kisses; the centers stay juicy. Checkpoint — pull when a test cube's cut face is opaque to the center with clear juices — no pink, chicken's absolute rule — and the scallion has gone sweet-soft with char at the edges.
  3. Off the skewer at the table or straight off the stick; sesame over; cho-gochujang (#SA03) as the dip. Checkpoint — eating test: chicken and scallion from the same bite, garlic-soy glaze behind them, then the dip's sweet-heat on the finish. The stick is the portion — two skewers and rice is the honest serving.

Storage, Make-Ahead & Variations

- Skewers marinate 24 hours maximum, threads and all. Cooked, 3 days — pull the meat off for a rice bowl or a wrap; the stick has done its work. - Variations: thread mushrooms between the cubes for the half-vegetarian table; the gochujang marinade fork (#M17's paste on cubes); beef sirloin cubes take the same build and grill a minute faster.
