Gochujang-Glazed Grilled Salmon

Prep: 15 minutes, plus 30 minutes resting · Cook: 10 minutes · Total: About 55 minutes · Serves 4

Ingredients

The salmon

  • 4 salmon fillets (about 700 g), skin on, pin bones pulled, room temperature

The glaze

  • 2 tbsp gochujang (about 40 g)
  • 1.5 tbsp honey (about 30 g)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce (15 ml)
  • 1 tbsp mirin (15 ml)
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil (5 ml)
  • 2 cloves garlic (about 8 g), grated
  • 1 tsp gochugaru (about 3 g), optional

The finish and table

  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds (about 8 g), sliced scallion (about 10 g)
  • Steamed rice (about 600 g cooked), sesame-soy cucumber (#B10's lane — any quick cucumber banchan), perilla leaves

Instructions

  1. Skin-down on the moderate zone, oiled grate, 5–6 minutes undisturbed — the skin crisps in its own rendered fat exactly the way the duck skin does in #M19, and the skin is the platform: pull the fillet early elsewhere but never on the skin. Checkpoint — the skin releases clean and crisp-gold at the edges; the flesh at the sides has turned opaque a third of the way up the fillet.
  2. Flip once, 2 minutes on the flesh side, then the glaze: brush, 45 seconds, second thin brush-stroke, and pull at the table's preferred center — salmon's window is wide, from just-translucent at 50 °C to opaque-flaky at 58 °C, and this glaze suits the juicier end. Checkpoint — a test flake at the thick end: it parts along the grain with no force, still glossy inside, glaze set brick-tacky on the outside. If the glaze blackens, the hot zone ran past its sugar — the fish is fine underneath; notch the fire down for the second set.
  3. Sesame and scallion over, platter with the cucumber banchan and perilla beside. Checkpoint — eating test: glaze edge, salmon's sweet fat, the cucumber's cold snap between bites — rich, spiced, reset. A piece of the crisp skin with the glaze is the record's best single bite, and the cook's by right.

Storage, Make-Ahead & Variations

- Raw fillets keep 1 day; the glaze keeps a week refrigerated and improves. Cooked, 2 days — cold flaked salmon over rice with the last of the glaze thinned as a sauce. - Variations: the doenjang-glaze fork for the mellower table (#SF11); the same glaze on the black cod at its slower clock; broiler path — skin-down on a sheet pan, top rack, glaze in the last 3 minutes — for the no-grill weeknight.
