**Stream: restaurant, the yangnyeom lane.** Plain language first, per this site's standing rule: gochujang-marinated beef is not "bulgogi with gochujang." It is the yangnyeom — "seasoned" — lane, a third architecture with its own logic: the paste both marinates and glazes, the heat sits over sweetness instead of beside it, and the finished rib wears a red lacquer that regular soy marinade never gives. This record takes the LA-cut flanken strip (#M03) into that lane. Same cut, same grill choreography, different country of flavor.

Spicy Gochujang Galbi

Prep: 20 minutes, plus 6–24 hours marinating · Cook: 12 minutes · Total: About 35 minutes at the table (marinate ahead) · Serves 4

Ingredients

The ribs

  • 1.6 kg beef short ribs, flanken-cut across the bone, 8–10 mm thick, lightly crosshatch scored

The marinade-glaze

  • 4 tbsp gochujang (about 80 g)
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce (45 ml)
  • 2 tbsp honey or brown sugar (about 40 g)
  • 2 tbsp mirin (30 ml)
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil (15 ml)
  • 6 cloves garlic (about 24 g), grated
  • 1 thumb ginger (about 15 g), grated
  • 1/2 Asian pear (about 150 g), grated and squeezed nearly dry
  • 2 tsp gochugaru (about 6 g), for the ceiling-chasers
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds (about 8 g), to finish

The table

  • Steamed rice (about 600 g cooked), lettuce ssam, cold oi muchim (#B07) and kimchi (#B01) — the fresh, cold banchan matters more here, the ribs run hot

Instructions

  1. Charcoal or cast-iron at medium-hot — you can hold your hand over it 3–4 seconds. The gochujang sugars scorch faster than soy marinade, so build a cool zone or keep the grate a hand higher than for #M03. Bone side down first, single layer. Checkpoint — the paste should sizzle quietly and smell toasted-chile-sweet, not burnt. Any black smoke in the first minute means the fire is too close — raise the grate before the first flip, not after the first casualty.
  2. Grill 3–4 minutes a side. The visual law changes in this lane: you are not waiting for mahogany, you are waiting for the red paste to darken one shade toward brick and go glossy at the edges. Flip once at the weep; pull the strip when the cut edge at the bone shows no raw red. Checkpoint — the pull test: brick-red lacquer, glossy, light char in the score marks, meat firm at the bone with a pink sliver allowed. Eat one piece at the grill station — the heat should land after the sweet, never before it.
  3. Scissors to single-bone pieces, warm platter, sesame over, straight into ssam with rice and a piece of cold cucumber banchan in the same bite's orbit. Checkpoint — eating test: sweet, then warm heat, then beef — and the cold oi muchim resets the palate between ssam. If the heat erases the beef, the gochujang ran over 80 g; weigh it next time.

Troubleshooting

Marinade slid offWet pear or wet ribsSqueeze the pear; pat strips dry before coating

Storage, Make-Ahead & Variations

- Marinated ribs keep 24 hours. Cooked, 3 days — chop off the bone into fried rice or a quesadilla, where the residual lacquer does the seasoning. Bones to the stock pot. - Variations: the soy-pear parent (#M03); pork shoulder in this same paste is the spicy dwaeji bulgogi record (#M13); thinned with a splash of water and a spoon of vinegar, the leftover paste becomes a brushable glaze for grilled shrimp (#SF01).
