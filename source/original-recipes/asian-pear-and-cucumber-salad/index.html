**Stream: heritage — the palate cleanser that grows on trees.** Asian pear — the crisp, watery, honeyed fruit that marinates meat in this cuisine's other records — takes the leading role here: matchsticked with cucumber and dressed in a light sweet-soy-sesame with a gochugaru thread. It is the fresh bowl for the heaviest grill meals, the one that resets a palate loaded with gochujang (#M13) and lacquer (#M03), and the only bowl on the fresh list that belongs to dessert's register while staying firmly at dinner.

Asian Pear and Cucumber Salad

Prep: 15 minutes · Cook: 0 minutes · Total: 15 minutes, serve within the hour · Serves 4 as banchan

Ingredients

The fruit and vegetable

  • 1 large Asian pear (about 400 g), cored, cut into matchsticks (skin on — the skin is the texture)
  • 1 cucumber (about 200 g), cut into matchsticks
  • A pinch of coarse salt (about 2 g), over the cucumber only, 5 minutes

The dressing

  • 1 tbsp soy sauce (15 ml)
  • 1.5 tsp honey (about 10 g)
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar (15 ml)
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil (15 ml)
  • 1/2 tsp gochugaru (about 3 g), optional thread
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds (about 4 g)
  • Black pepper; 1 scallion (about 15 g), sliced

Instructions

  1. The dressing whisked light — soy, honey, vinegar, sesame oil, gochugaru — thinner than the family's other dressings: this salad's dressing is a coat, not a sauce. Checkpoint — tastes delicate: sweet first, a light soy savor, citrus-edge acid, a faint warm thread. A strong dressing here overwhelms the pear; restraint is the recipe.
  2. Pear, squeezed cucumber, and scallion folded through with spoons — thirty seconds, no more — sesame and pepper over. Checkpoint — the eating test between the grill's richest bites: cold, wet, crisp, faintly sweet, cleaned. The bowl's whole job is the word "cleaned," and beside the black cod (#SF11) or the galbi (#M03) it performs that job completely.

Storage, Make-Ahead & Variations

- Eats within the hour; there is no keeping. Dressing keeps a week in a jar. - Variations: matchstick jujube or a few pine nuts over; the yuja dressing (#F05's) swapped for the citrus fork; the same build over shaved ice becomes dessert's first step toward #D02.
